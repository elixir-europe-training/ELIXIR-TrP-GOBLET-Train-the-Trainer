## ELIXIR-GOBLET Train-the-Trainer - Extended Lesson Plan

This is a more granular version of [lesson_plan.md](lesson_plan.md), breaking each day down to the level of individual Parts and Challenges, using the real Challenge timings confirmed against the shared-notes document.

{% capture important_1 %}
Only Challenge timings are measured, real data. **Lecture/explanation durations for each Part are estimates**, calculated from each Part's word count in the handbook, scaled to fill whatever time is left after subtracting all Challenge time from the available window. This gives a defensible starting point for planning and for placing coffee breaks at natural stopping points - but it is not a measured fact. Adjust freely based on your own experience actually delivering the material.

Coffee breaks are placed at the natural Part/Challenge boundary closest to an even division of the day's content - not at fixed clock times. This sometimes means segments between breaks are not exactly equal length.
{% endcapture %}
